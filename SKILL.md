# Amazon Keyword Research

## Core Workflow
1. Start with seed keywords from your product
2. Use Amazon auto-suggest to expand
3. Analyze volume vs competition
4. Target long-tail keywords first
5. Fill backend search terms (250 bytes x5 fields)

## Quick Tips
- Prioritize keywords with high relevance + low competition
- Include synonyms, misspellings in backend terms
- Don't repeat title words in backend
